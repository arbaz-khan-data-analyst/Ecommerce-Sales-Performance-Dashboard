📊 E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard | Built 100% in Excel

I'm excited to share my latest project: an interactive E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard built entirely in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers.

🔍 About the Project
I analyzed a dataset of 9,994 orders (2011-2014) covering customer segments, regions, states, product categories, and sub-categories, and turned it into a single-page dashboard for quick business decisions.

📌 Key Insights
💰 Total Sales: $2.29M (YoY growth: +20.62%)
📈 Total Profit: $286K (YoY growth: +14.41%)
📦 Quantity Sold: 37,873 units (YoY growth: +27.45%)
🧾 Total Orders: 9,994 (YoY growth: +28.64%)
📉 Profit Margin: 12.47%, though it declined year over year, which flags an area to improve

🛠️ What's Inside the Dashboard
✅ KPI Cards with YoY growth tracking
✅ Combo Chart: monthly Sales vs Profit trend
✅ Waterfall Chart: category-wise profit contribution
✅ Donut Chart: category-wise sales share %
✅ Map Chart: state-wise sales across the US
✅ Top 5 Sub-Categories: Phones, Chairs, Storage, Tables, Binders
✅ Interactive Slicers: filter by Year, Region, and Segment

💡 Key Takeaways
- Sales and profit peak in the later months of the year (Sep-Dec), showing clear seasonality.
- Technology and Furniture lead in sales share, while Phones and Chairs are the top-selling sub-categories.
- Sales are strongest in states like California and New York.
- Sales grew strongly YoY, but the profit margin dipped, so there's room to optimize costs and discounts.
